# n1 - bigint safe wire codec

`src/signer/protocol.ts`: `SIGNER_PROTOCOL_VERSION`, `encodeJson`, `decodeJson`. Bigints are
tagged as `{"$bigint":"1000"}` and revived as bigint, or rejected if the digits are not an integer.

Why tagging rather than plain strings: the captured EIP-712 payload carries `value`, `validAfter`
and `validBefore` as bigint. `JSON.stringify` throws on those, so a naive codec fails loudly, but a
codec that encoded them as strings would fail silently by making viem hash a different value and
sign a valid signature over the wrong payment.

The gate is the fourth test: a round tripped payload must sign to byte identical output.
