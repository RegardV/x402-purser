# n4 - the daemon side wallet

`src/socket-wallet.ts`: `connectSignerWallet(socketPath): Promise<Wallet>`.

Async because `Wallet.address` is a plain property rather than a method, so the address has to be
fetched once at connect time. That is why the signer protocol carries an `address` op at all.

`lock()` is deliberately local only. Forwarding it would hand a compromised daemon a way to disable
signing for every other consumer, turning a containment feature into a denial of service handle.
Stopping the signer process is the real revocation.

4 tests, including the byte identical check through a live socket and the assertion that a policy
refusal arrives as a thrown error rather than as a malformed signature.
